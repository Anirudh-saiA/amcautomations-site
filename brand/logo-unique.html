Three original logo directions

Each is drawn from scratch around the three letters A, M and C and the idea of work flowing from one step to the next, with one blue dot as the signature. Shown on white, on dark and at tiny sizes.

1A with a node

The idea: the round, single-storey a from the wordmark, standing alone. A blue dot floats above the stem: the one step the automation has just handed on. It is the icon for the site, the app tab and social profiles, and it matches the wordmark exactly.

2Signal wordmark

The idea: the name drawn as even-weight rounded lowercase letters. The c is left open, and its tip ends in a blue dot, like one step handing over to the next. No symbol is needed, and it can sit on its own on the site, on cards and in email.

3Peaks icon

The idea: the M drawn as two peaks, which are also two A shapes. The blue dot sits at the valley where two sides meet, because that is what consulting and automation do: join the people and the process. Best as an app icon, profile picture and favicon.
