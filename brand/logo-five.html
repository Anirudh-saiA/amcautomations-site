AMC Automations: five logo directions

Each one is shown on beige and on black, with small-size tests. The tagline under every logo is AI · Media · Commerce. All colours come from palette v1. Concept sketches only, nothing here is on the website.

1The Loop A my pick

A capital A whose crossbar is a ring. The ring is a cycle: work that runs and repeats by itself. It is still just a letter, so it is easy to remember and easy to draw.

2Three Dots, One Line

Blue, orange and green dots joined by one line, forming a pointing-up A. The dots are AI, Media and Commerce, and the line is the automation that links them. It matches the name best, but it is the most generic of the five.

3Play-A

4Switch

A pill with the knob flipped to ON. It says "on autopilot" without a single word, and it is friendly but not cartoony. The mark carries no letter, so the name has to do the work.

5Monogram Block

A solid rounded square with a bold A and one orange dot, next to a heavy wordmark. It is the most serious of the five and the best for invoices, proposals and cards. It has the least personality.
